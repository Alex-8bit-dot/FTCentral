FTCentral
Dashboard Scouting Events Messages Learning

FTCentral is made by the MantaBots, FTC Team 27318 at the French-American School of New York.

Team, match and event data comes from FTC Scout, which uses the official FIRST API. Match videos are from FTC Match Videos.

FIRST®, FIRST® Tech Challenge and BIOBUZZ™ are trademarks of FIRST. FTCentral is a student project and is not run or endorsed by FIRST.

Your privacy

FTCentral has no accounts, ads or tracking. Your profile, ratings, notes and message drafts are saved only in this browser. The only thing sent anywhere is the team or event you look up, which goes to FTC Scout.
